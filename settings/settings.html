Smart Freezing

Automatically suspend idle tabs, guarded by smart safety checks.

Enable Smart Freezing Master switch: auto-suspend idle tabs with the checks below. Turning it off keeps your individual check settings.
Form, media, and activity detection read page content and need the optional page-access permission.

Protect Playing Media Don't suspend tabs with any playing audio or video element, even muted ones
Protect Meeting Sites Don't suspend Meet, Zoom, Teams, Discord, and other call sites
Protect Music Sites Don't suspend Spotify, YouTube Music, SoundCloud, and other music players
Protect Forms Don't suspend pages with typed form data, changed dropdowns, rich-text edits, or an unsaved-changes warning
Protect Live-Updating Pages Don't suspend pages whose title keeps changing (dashboards, trackers, live scores)
Respect Page Activity Typing, mouse movement, or scrolling in a page resets its idle timer

Tab Protection

Prevent certain types of tabs from being suspended.

Protect Media Tabs Don't suspend tabs playing audible audio or video
Protect Pinned Tabs Don't suspend pinned tabs
Protect Active Tabs Never suspend the active tab of any window
Protect Local URLs Don't suspend localhost, 127.0.0.1, file://, or private network addresses
Auto Restore on Activate Automatically restore a suspended tab when you click on it
